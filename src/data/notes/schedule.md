- **Dinner & Leisure**: 6:30 PM - 8:30 PM

## Productivity Rules & Time Hacks
- Use time-blocking for deep focus work.
- listen to music while working
- Keep responses and task updates concise.
- Minimize distractions during deep focus hours.
